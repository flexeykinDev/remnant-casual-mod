## Description

```
[size=4]Casual Mod[/size]

Remnant is a great game to look at and a long game to grind. This mod keeps the worlds, the bosses and
the builds, and removes the grind around them. Made for short sessions, sandbox runs and speedruns.

[b]Loot[/b]
- Scrap, iron and ammo x100
- Lumenite crystals and simulacrums: 100 per drop instead of 1-8, always drop
- Normal enemies (soldiers and swarms) always drop loot
- Merchant stock x100

[b]Fights[/b]
- Enemy health 50%, enemy damage 40%, on every difficulty
- Player: 250 health, double stamina, 3x faster stamina regen, 300 reserve ammo
- Weapon damage, clip size and reserve ammo x2
- Dragon Heart heals to full and starts with 10 charges

[b]Builds[/b]
- Every trait: level-1 value and per-level growth x3, max level 60 instead of 20
- Elder Knowledge and Scavenger x10, Sagestone +500% XP, Scavenger's Bauble +500% scrap
- All 130 rings and amulets: every positive bonus x2, downsides untouched
- Weapon mods (F): charge 4x faster, damage/shields/summons x2, durations x2, cooldowns halved
- Experience x5

[b]Boss weapon mods for sale[/b]
- The Ward 13 merchant stocks 12 mods that are welded into boss weapons in vanilla (Undying, Hive Shot,
  Gravity Core, Flamethrower, Skewer, Vampiric and more), so any weapon can carry one
- Warning: the game still treats them as built-in. A boss mod cannot be taken off again and is destroyed
  when you unequip that weapon, so choose the weapon before you install it
- These entries replace the iron, lumenite and simulacrum the merchant used to sell - all of it drops x100 now

[b]Comfort[/b]
- Food buffs last 10 hours, potions 10x longer, effects x2
- Armor has no weight, so dodges stay fast in any set
- Prices, upgrade and crafting costs at 10%, items sell for the full price

[b]Install[/b]
1. Drop CasualMod_P.pak into ...\steamapps\common\Remnant\Remnant\Content\Paks
2. Start the game
3. To uninstall, delete the file. Saves keep working.

Singleplayer or hosting only - a client without the mod will not see these values.

[b]Notes[/b]
- Does not touch boss health scaling, enemy counts or anything outside the values listed above
- Built with an open-source toolkit, every value is a setting: <github link>

Made by flexeykinDEV
```
